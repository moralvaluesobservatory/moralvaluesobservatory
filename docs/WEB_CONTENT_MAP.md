# Web content map

The public site is served from `docs/index.html`.

## 1. Governance question

Explain the normative-concentration problem and state that the current release
is a methodological pilot, not a completed representation audit.

## 2. From pilots to an observatory

Describe the progression from exploratory instruments to correction,
validation, and cumulative case studies.

## 3. Current case study — DSE

Use only active outputs from `data/results/active/`:

- merit-related effects in the workplace task;
- financial-need effects in the inheritance task;
- exact equal splits compared with selected published human point estimates;
- visible limits on inference.

## 4. What the pilot does not establish

Representation, disagreement compression, generalization, longitudinal
stability, multilingual equivalence, and moral competence remain open.

## 5. What next

The next experiment is the FAW Attitudes Audit based on Almås et al. (2022),
followed by an intensive robustness module and a later FAIR impartial-spectator
allocation study. Keep the 2022 attitudes dataset and the 2025 experiment
clearly distinct.

## 6. Evidence to date

Present external literature as methodological starting points, not as a
permanent exercise in comparing every project result with other publications.

## 7. Research development

Preserve pilot findings, corrections, excluded configurations, and withdrawn or
inconclusive claims.

## 8. Human coverage

Show who is visible in current references, what the planned FAW microdata add,
and which populations or within-country groups remain unmeasured.

## 9. Methods and research record

Link the versioned package, checksum, statistical-unit rules, exact
configurations, bibliography, and licenses.
